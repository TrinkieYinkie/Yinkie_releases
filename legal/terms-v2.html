Operator and service

Yinkie is a local Markdown editor maintained by Alisa Petrova (Петрова Алиса), an individual based in Georgia. There is currently no company, account, payment service or cloud document-sync service. The first public Desktop distribution is a Windows x64 prerelease. Features and platform availability may change.

Your files and use of the editor

You keep ownership of your documents. Using Yinkie does not transfer that ownership to the maintainer. Use the editor for content you are entitled to process and comply with applicable law. Keep independent backups of important files: recovery drafts and local history are useful safeguards, not a guarantee against all data loss or a substitute for your own backup.

Optional network features

Product analytics requires a separate explicit choice under the Privacy Policy. Accepting these Terms or using the editor never enables analytics. Remote images have separate per-image permission. Desktop update checks contact GitHub independently of analytics consent and offer a release-page link; they do not silently install updates. The Privacy Policy describes the information associated with these requests.
